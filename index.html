Common Questions

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Flatiron, NoMad, Gramercy, and surrounding neighborhoods in Manhattan. Route density is how we keep quality high and pricing fair.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No long-term contracts. All programs are month-to-month with 30 days notice. We earn your business every week.

Do I need to provide planters or vessels?

No. We provide, maintain, and rotate all planters and vessels as part of your program. Nothing for you to buy or store.

What do programs cost?

It depends on the scope. A focused plant program starts around $500/month. A full environment program with plants, florals, and seasonal styling typically runs $1,500 to $3,000/month. Every quote is based on a walkthrough of your specific space.
